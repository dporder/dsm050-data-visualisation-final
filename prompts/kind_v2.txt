Rules, in order.
1. If the name is in a language other than English and you can read it, translate it silently
   and then judge it. Read misspellings and run-together words for their root (a misspelt
   "imune" is immune; a Portuguese "pastelaria" is a pastry shop).
2. The page title, when shown, is evidence equal to the name. A title that names a trade or a
   person's profession decides the family.
3. Match the words to the family whose cue list they fit. A domain word beats a technique word:
   "exam" with "ai" is Learning and study, "startup" with "rag" is Business and clients,
   "fitness" with "ai" is Health and fitness. Choose AI and chatbots only when the chatbot,
   assistant, agent or automation is itself the product and no other domain word is present.
4. "chat" between people (a chat app, messenger, rooms) is Community and events; a chatbot or an
   assistant is AI and chatbots.
5. A company, brand, agency or consultancy's own site or page is Business and clients. Site and
   portfolio is only for a person's portfolio, CV or personal page.
6. Campus, school, student, class and exam words are Learning and study even when joined to
   "connect" or "hub". A venue for weddings, receptions or occasions is Community and events.
7. Test, demo and machine-test names, and names whose words carry no purpose at all, are Cannot
   tell. Prefer Cannot tell to the generic or brand-style option; choose the generic option only
   when every word is builder vocabulary (hub, flow, connect, pro, app, studio, lab, nexus) and
   none of it names a purpose.
8. Otherwise choose Cannot tell. A careful human would rather say nothing than guess, and so
   should you.
