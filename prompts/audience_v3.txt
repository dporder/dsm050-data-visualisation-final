The two sectors come from the free-innovation literature (von Hippel, de Jong and Flowers
2012; von Hippel 2017). Household sector: made for the maker, their family, friends, a club
or a community, at private cost and not for sale. Producer sector: made for a business, for
clients, or to be sold or charged for.

Rules, in order.
1. If the name is in a language other than English and you can read it, translate it silently
   and then judge it. Read the page title, when shown, as evidence equal to the name.
2. Choose the producer sector when the words name a business or trade of any kind: a shop,
   restaurant, bakery or pastry shop, a salon, a fitness studio or gym as a business, an agency,
   a consultancy, a construction firm, a "digital", "solutions" or "automations" business, a
   startup, a brand offering a service, a venue or booking service, a marketplace, a recruitment
   or staffing service, or a business function (HR, CRM, sales, vendors, funnels, insight and
   analytics for a business, gig work for customers, client tasks). A company's own page or site
   is producer sector, including a "page" or "site" named for a brand word that is not a
   person's name. A person offering a specialist service (a coach, trainer, whisperer,
   consultant) is producer sector, and so is a service that supplies helpers, hands or workers
   for tasks.
3. Choose the household sector only when the words themselves name a community group,
   nonprofit, foundation, charity, club, church, neighbourhood or town, a free public resource
   for a place (a city's spots or public facilities), or the maker's own family, home or
   friends. A campus, school, class or student app without such a word is cannot tell, not
   household: a campus is an institution, not a community the maker belongs to.
4. Choose cannot tell when the words name a personal-use app with no business or community
   named: a budget, habit, routine, sleep, fitness, wellness, mood, calm, chat, chatbot,
   assistant, quiz, game, test or demo app. A personal portfolio, CV or personal site is cannot
   tell: it promotes a person, which is neither sector by itself.
5. Otherwise choose cannot tell. A careful human would rather say nothing than guess, and so
   should you.
